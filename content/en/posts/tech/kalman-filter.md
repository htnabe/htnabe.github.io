## Transistor Gijutsu, July 2019 Issue

I had never heard of this magazine before it was recommended to me, but apparently it's a legendary, long-running publication in the electronics industry ([founded October 1964](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%88%E3%83%A9%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B8%E3%82%B9%E3%82%BF%E6%8A%80%E8%A1%93)).

The July 2019 issue features a special on probabilistic computing inspired by the Apollo lunar lander, and covers Kalman filter-based control of an inverted pendulum system in thorough detail — right down to individual component descriptions. **This single issue covers everything from theory to hands-on experimentation.**

I won't explain the Kalman filter itself here (intellectual property concerns), but personally, thanks to this magazine, I feel like I now understand it well enough to explain it to others.

## Summary

Given that **accessible explanations of the Kalman filter are hard to come by** (at least I hadn't found any), a Japanese-language resource that patiently explains all the required background knowledge this thoroughly is genuinely rare.

And it's just ¥1,100 including tax, shipping excluded — an absurdly good deal. Highly recommended for everyone from beginners to veterans!

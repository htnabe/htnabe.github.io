## Current Playtime and Story Progress

Current playtime is 45 hours, and as for progress, I've finished the final boss (main-story boss) and defeated each fairy of the five attributes.

Apparently there's also a hidden boss, but I haven't reached that yet.

So yeah, since I've basically cleared things and reached a nice stopping point, I'll write the review now.

### Early Game

{{<youtube npJyUVi6HoQ>}}

Right after starting, the OP above plays, and up to that point I was like "oh, this looks fun" (past tense).

But once I actually started playing, although the scenery is beautiful, compared with AAA RPGs I felt the rough quality of NPC design and BGM stood out. I could tolerate BGM somewhat, but the reused NPCs were honestly too much...

Also after actually playing, I found the gameplay, messy UI feel, and super long tutorial all strongly gave off old-school ("good old"?) JRPG vibes, and I figured players in the Reiwa era wouldn't rate that highly. Maybe barely okay in Heisei. In Showa it would've been accepted.

Also, in the OP above, Ampel and Lila are shown as if they oppose Ryza and the others, but this is basically just there to mislead players and is almost unrelated to the story ww ~~I seriously don't understand why they put this in~~

Still, the camera mode available from early game was pretty fun.

Like below, imagining the heroines doing chatty sidewalk meetings and grinning while using camera mode made me lose a full hour like "1 hour just melted^^" pretty often.

### Late Game

Lila-neki proposes the reckless idea of going to defeat the witch. Well there's probably no other way, but I was thinking "this smells like a trap," "are Lila and the others actually masterminds after all...?" and "maybe Kilo really is the Queen of Scorching Darkness?" ~~Not even 1mm true though~~

From around here, a broken item called the **Space-Time Astronomical Clock** appears.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1672744468/daily/games/atelier-ryza/tokei0_dkqofn.webp" alt="Space-Time Astronomical Clock">}}

In this game's combat, isn't it insanely important how quickly you raise Tactics Level and how long you can keep the enemy in Break state? And Break is easy to trigger just by raising Tactics Level and hitting physically, so in practice raising Tactics Level is top priority.

So when a broken item appears that raises Tactics Level fairly easily at CC5 and also boosts parameters plus shortens WT, all you can do is laugh. Power inflation has begun (certain).

From when I got the Space-Time Astronomical Clock, I progressed the story smoothly without losses, and this part was normally fun as a game.

And, as usual, there's the camera mode.

### Boss Battle

Turns out Ampel and Kilo were both allies after all.

Boss fight............I lost in seconds. That boss was busted, impossible to win this.

I figured I probably wouldn't win first try, but boss firepower was way beyond expectation and I laughed.

For revenge, I alchemy-overhauled **party armor (Ryza, Klaudia, Lent)** + **Space-Time Astronomical Clock**, boosted defense hard, raised level to around 55, and won pretty easily ww

Boss BGM was great and gave a hype feel. My only regret is that we never learned the final boss's true identity (face, etc.).

Some people may lose interest because late game becomes a Space-Time Astronomical Clock game, but personally I had fun doing the whole I'M SO STRONG thing (small-sample comment).

## Overall Review

### Good Points

- You can play with camera mode in many ways (really many)
- Characters are cute
- Late-game item/weapon power inflation feel
- Some enemies become easier by utilizing alchemy (alchemy system doesn't become dead weight)

### Bad Points

- BGM/3D models/NPC designs lacking polish
- Cluttered UI that's hard to operate
- Thin story with no major twist (plus weirdly suggestive OP)
- Frequent map travel + many movie scenes cause dragging

### Mixed Point

The complexity of the alchemy system isn't bad itself, but it's so complex that player preference will definitely split. Especially early game, when you can't craft many items yet, it can feel needlessly complicated.

By the end, unlocking many items started feeling fun (like collecting items), so I personally liked that part.

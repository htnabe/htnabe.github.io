- Type: Countertop dishwasher/dryer
- Size feel: Compact (width x height x depth = 42cm x 44.5cm x 43.5cm)

## After 4 Months of Use (Detailed Experience)

### Demerits

This is probably common among many devices with drying features, but **plastic containers don't dry very well**. Right now I take them out after drying and let them air-dry every time.

### Merits

The biggest point in real use is reduced mental burden from dishwashing, and **it seriously, genuinely dropped** :joy_cat:.

Even on busy days, it changed from "ugh, I have to wash dishes..." to "it's okay if I do it later," and that is huge of the year. So far I often use it in batches once or twice a week.

At first I wasn't doing this much, but after realizing a 20cm frying pan also fits, I started washing pans in it too. Just in case, when grease is heavy, I wipe off the oil once before putting it in.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1764479452/daily/%E9%A3%9F%E6%B4%97%E5%99%A8/IMG20251130133418_wzbvpm.jpg" alt="Figure of trying a frying pan too" caption="Tried fitting a frying pan too">}}

## Notes

You may need to be careful about noise and vibration. In rooms with thin walls, it might carry to neighboring rooms. It didn't feel like a rubber mat could really solve it.

For detergent, you need one for dishwashers (compatible with drying function). I use the one below.

{{<amazon asin="B0CF4XRB1V" title="Joy PRO Dishwasher Detergent Gel Tab 48 pieces">}}

Also, after washing, open it to dry. It drains through the drain hose, but water often remains at the bottom of the unit, so I leave it open after every use.

## Summary

Personally, this removed dishwashing, which was my biggest private-life stress, so it's god of gods. For me, **it's definitely this year's best buy**.

I want basically all humans to get one if they feel daily dishwashing is a burden: people living alone, dual-income households, or people who prefer washing in batches during the week (meal prep/lots of lunch boxes). It's also nice that you can install it quickly without construction.

## Story

As you can tell from the OP, it felt like **Daru + Chuutane Robo Club vs Kou Kimijima**.

{{< youtube kt76GWtOneA >}}

Focusing only on story, honestly outside Akiho Phase and Last Phase, I couldn't shake the feeling that the narrative was padded out because it had to be. If you expected dense story gimmicks like Steins;Gate, this might feel unsatisfying.

In my case, I mainly wanted to know how Robono concludes after the anime, so I enjoyed the different routes normally.

Overall, as a robot SF title, I think **it wasn't that bad, right?**

On the flip side, even players coming from the anime can play DaSH without much discomfort, which may be a good point (though game controls are honestly meh).

Across the whole story, for better or worse, you can strongly feel the vibe of **we're going to force a happy ending no matter what**. Evaluation of that point likely changes a lot depending on what you expect from this title.

Also, unlike Robono ELITE or the Steins;Gate series, this game has no bad endings, so it's playable with less tension, and many people may be disappointed by the relatively thin story. If you're about to play, better not set expectations too high.

Thoughts on each route:

- **Junna Route**
  Clumsy-cute let's go. Only the final vs Subaru fight was really hype.

- **Subaru Route**
  Fujoshi-oriented. A route with many moments where I felt "what am I even being shown..." and where your love for the content is tested.

- **Airi Route**
  Is this... a good story maybe? Airi-taso prpr...prprprprprprpr...prprprpr...~~phew~~

- **Frau Route**
  Eh... Frau-taso deserved to be happier... good night

- **Akiho Route**
  Super sweet. Yes, more of this. Overwhelming heroine energy.

- **Daru Route**
  A typical convenient plot. But that's exactly why it's good! ~~If you're the type who can say that, you'll enjoy it~~

So yeah, that's the feeling. In the final Daru-route scene, the way multiple copies of Kou Kimijima appeared and vanished was shown in a pretty suggestive way, and I'm curious whether that connects to a next title (if there is one) or was just direction.

If they make a next entry, I hope they deliver a denser story.

## Controls / Bonus Videos etc.

Many players mention this, but I feel poor controls cast a big shadow over the game.

- **No mouse support** despite being a PC game

This feels like a clear production-side issue, doesn't it? I'm pretty dissatisfied with this point too.

Also personally, **the effect when transitioning to flashback scenes was very stressful**.

Even when the flow made it obvious a flashback was coming, **the slow fade-out effect every single time** felt too roundabout and really irritated me.

Lastly, though it's already on YouTube, the video bonus was personally top-tier awesome. It felt exactly like **yes yes, this is exactly what we wanted**, content everyone could enjoy.

{{< youtube Cg9SR6yYHQo >}}

Though personally I prefer Frau-taso solo MV more (self-indulgent comment).

### Update

At some point keyboard control became available. But gamepad is recommended. It seems to fall back to keyboard controls if you don't have a gamepad.

## Summary

1. (If unplayed) It's best to play without over-expecting.
1. Daru route wasn't bad. The final battle was really hype.

Now I'm seriously thinking about what game to play next.

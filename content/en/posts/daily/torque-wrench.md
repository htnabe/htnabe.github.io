## Whether You Need a Digital Torque Wrench Depends on You

Of course, a digital torque wrench is nice to have — it lets you monitor the current torque in real time, which makes maintaining your bike more safely. That said, the necessity of a digital torque wrench really depends on factors like how tight the torque tolerance is for the part you're working on, and how well you can use a preset-type ratchet torque wrench.

Looking specifically at the tightening torque for sprockets, the installation torque for the 105 sprocket (CS-5700) I use is 30 Nm ~ 50 Nm (see the official [instruction manual](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/1YN0A/SI-1YN0A-001-ENG.pdf)).

If you tighten to 40 Nm, and the wrench has ±4% accuracy, that gives you 38.4 Nm ~ 41.6 Nm — well within the specified range, no problem.

However, if a part had a spec of, say, 38 Nm ~ 42 Nm (whether that's realistic or not), you'd be pretty nervous tightening to 40 Nm with the above wrench…

For cases like that, or especially where overtightening could cause damage, a digital torque wrench for more precise tightening would be the right choice.

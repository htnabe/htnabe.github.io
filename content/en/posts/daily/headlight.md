What I bought this time is the GENTOS LED Headlight (MAX 300 lumens). The official website is [here](https://www.gentos.jp/products/series/auva/va-05d).

I recently wanted to go running early in the morning and at night, but the ground was too hard to see and it was really scary, so I bought this.

I've only used it 5 times over the two weeks since buying it, but so far it's been a really good purchase, so I'll write a review.

## Main Specifications

- Light source: 1 white LED + 1 warm-color LED
- Body weight (approx.): 81g (batteries included)
- Battery: 2 AAA batteries
- Range of motion: 80°
- Water resistance: 1m waterproof (IP66, IP67 compliant)
- Maximum beam distance: 66m
- Brightness and runtime

{{<table "table">}}
|                      |            |            |           |                 |
| -------------------- | ---------- | ---------- | --------- | :-------------: |
| Brightness (approx.) | 300 lumens | 100 lumens | 30 lumens | 6 lumens (warm) |
| Runtime (approx.)    | 3.5 hrs    | 10 hrs     | 30 hrs    |     74 hrs      |
{{</table>}}

Those are the key specs. Incidentally, the IP67 water resistance rating means it won't leak even if submerged 1m deep for 30 minutes[^1]. But the actual brightness, beam range, comfort, and belt length are hard to gauge from specs alone...

So let me explain in more detail.

### Actual Brightness and Beam Range

First, 300 lumens.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1670677728/daily/head-light/300lumen_ivuwnm.webp" alt="picture_of_300lumen">}}

At 300 lumens it's quite bright, lighting up roughly 2 lanes' width to about 10 meters ahead. It's bright enough to walk comfortably even on roads without streetlights.

Next, 100 lumens.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1670677929/daily/head-light/100lumen_jidgp1.webp" alt="picture_of_100lumen">}}

Darker than 300 lumens, but seems bright enough for roads with streetlights. Beam range is roughly 1 lane wide, reaching about 5 meters ahead.

Next, 30 lumens.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1670678113/daily/head-light/30lumen_mxycz0.webp" alt="picture_of_30lumen">}}

At this level you can really only light up the ground right at your feet. It seems right for use in somewhat-bright conditions around sunrise/sunset, or as a "hey, I'm here!" signal to let others know you're walking there.

Finally, 6 lumens wasn't even bright enough to photograph properly, so I'll skip it. It's just bright enough to light up your hands a little.

### Comfort

After about 5 running sessions, the non-slip pad on the inside of the band kept it from slipping even with sweat.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1670759347/daily/head-light/headlight_anti_slip_doqvqy.webp" alt="picture_of_belt">}}

Also, since you can freely adjust the band length, once you get it fitted to your head, it basically won't slip.

### Belt Length

It's on the longer side, but wrapping it around your waist would be difficult. Measured circumference was about 62cm.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1670759001/daily/head-light/headlight_length1_tp9nh7.webp" alt="length_of_belt1">}}

About 31cm on each side, so roughly 62cm around.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1670759001/daily/head-light/headlight_length2_u7rfxm.webp" alt="length_of_belt2">}}

### Battery Life

I average about an hour per run, so I charge the batteries every three runs or so. Thanks to that, I haven't run out of battery during a run yet. That said, the runtime isn't enormous, so forgetting to charge would be a little worrying... lol

## Overall

For a headlight you can buy for about ¥3,000 (as of December 2022), the specs are quite good and it's easy to use. Durability is a remaining question. I might review it again in six months or next year.

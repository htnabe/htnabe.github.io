The two main features of this stand are:

- It has a hole to pass a charging cable through
- It has anti-slip pads on both the bottom and the phone contact surface

## Final Thoughts

Right now I keep the smartphone stand on my desk like this. I used to place my phone directly on the desk, but thanks to this stand I now have a fixed place for it, and **the phenomenon of my phone disappearing inside the house has stopped**.

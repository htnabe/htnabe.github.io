## Background

Due to recent circumstances, it was decided that I would work entirely from home for the first half of this fiscal year.

I only had one desk at home — my PC gaming desk — and since **I'm not good at switching between ON/OFF mode, I knew I needed a quick way to separate my ON/OFF spaces**, so I decided to have a separate gaming desk and a work desk.

On top of that, I had some additional goals:

- A countermeasure against lack of exercise while working from home
- Not wanting to give in to various temptations

So I thought, why not try a height-adjustable desk ~~rumored to boost productivity~~...! (not my finest reasoning)

Also, during assembly there was one spot that was extremely difficult to adjust. In the finished state, pressing the pedal under the desk pulls a wire to move the height-adjustment section, but **adjusting the wire tension was incredibly difficult**...

I put it together once and it wouldn't move at all — turns out the wire wasn't tensioned enough, and it needed fine-tuning. The manual was way too sparse for a first-timer doing this lol.

**Anyway, assembly done!**

## After 2 Weeks of Use

Pros:

1. Wood grain smooth enough that nothing catches when writing documents
1. **The wooden tabletop blends naturally into the room without looking out of place**
1. At 120cm wide, there's plenty of room to work without feeling cramped
1. The curved front edge means you can lean on it without it being painful
1. The cable routing hole in the desk keeps things tidy whether you're using it or not

**Personally, the fact that it harmonizes with my room's design is a big plus.**

Cons:

1. Raising and lowering with the lever (especially raising) is a bit sluggish
1. I wish there was a cable management space under the desk for the power strip etc.

All in all, for me the pros far outweighed the cons, so it was definitely the right purchase~.

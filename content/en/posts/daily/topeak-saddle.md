- Tube

## The (Only) Downside

I've probably ridden somewhere between 500 km and 800 km with this saddle bag, and shortly after I started using it, a hole appeared in the back of the thighs on my cycling shorts[^2].

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1638089395/NabeBlog/TOPEAK_saddle_M_5_er0o7a.webp" alt="cycle_pants1">}}
{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1638089395/NabeBlog/TOPEAK_saddle_M_6_gt8cm9.webp" alt="cycle_pants">}}

I'm personally on the shorter side with fairly thick thighs, so people with a similar build might experience some rubbing. That said, it's more of a light graze, so it barely bothers me while pedaling.

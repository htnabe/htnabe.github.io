## After Using It for About 3 Weeks

I'd say the usability is "quite good" without hesitation. The only downside is that it's a bit thick, but that's easily solved with a monitor arm, so it's not really a dealbreaker.

For adjusting brightness and other settings, you can access various options via "Menu" ↓. The adjustment buttons are on the bottom edge of the display panel and work just fine.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1638104310/NabeBlog/LG_monitor12_kdbhcf.webp" alt="ajustor">}}

I personally dislike bright screens, and when I turned on the blue light reduction feature, the screen automatically dimmed to a comfortable level — perfect for me.

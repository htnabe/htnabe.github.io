## Introduction

I started hybrid work this year (remote 3-4 days/week), and I want to introduce the gadget case I've been using since then. This is after about half a year of use.

## First Impressions

At first I wanted to carry my personal/work smartphone charging cables, work smartphone, eye drops, etc., so I searched Amazon and picked one of the top results.

{{<amazon asin="B0DNDN1M8C" img_url="https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/41P+d89vjkL._AC_.jpg" title="Teskyer Gadget Case TC05BK (Black M)">}}

As listed on the product page, the size is 6.5cm x 14.5cm x 23cm (height x width x depth).

Thanks to my work smartphone being compact, it fits easily.

Currently I store chargers, smartphones, eye drops, and so on as shown below.

It has pockets for carrying SD cards and elastic bands to organize cables, but I didn't use them (unless you have a GoPro, you don't carry SD cards that often, right...?).

I took out everything I usually keep in it below.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1764416961/daily/%E3%82%AC%E3%82%B8%E3%82%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%E3%82%B1%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B9/IMG20251129202510_gq2bw8.jpg" alt="Figure of items taken out from gadget case" caption="Surprisingly, not that much stuff?">}}

My first impressions after starting to use it:

- (At first I had no plan to carry more than this) Everything fit and I was happy :smile:
- Slightly bulky size because it's a hard case
- Since it's split into two layers and can hold gadgets neatly, it reduced stress from tangled cables and small items going missing in my bag

### After 6 Months of Use

First, this strap that I thought "might be useful" at the time of purchase turned out to be something I never used at all. Maybe it makes sense if you attach it outside a bag.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1764416866/daily/%E3%82%AC%E3%82%B8%E3%82%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%E3%82%B1%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B9/IMG20251129202557_pu0odz.jpg" alt="Strap attached to the case" caption="There was a time I thought it would be handy">}}

Also, the biggest reason is that what I want to carry changed since I bought it, but now it's definitely too small :joy:

Especially when I try to carry a mouse: a slim mouse fits in the lower layer, but it may contact the smartphone or charger inside the case, so it seems I need to be careful about arrangement.

If I also want to carry a laptop charger, I probably need a larger case like this below (I might buy this one next).

{{<amazon asin="B09DK2GVB8" img_url="https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/319Kh5aS0iL._AC_.jpg" title="Sanwa Direct Gadget Pouch Semi-Hard with Divider, for Mouse/Cables/Mobile Battery, Black, LL Size, 200-BAGIN023BK">}}

## Summary

As a product, I think it's not bad at all.

- Very affordable
- Doesn't get too bulky even in business bags/backpacks, and easy to use across commuting, business trips, and travel
- Calm design that works in business scenes
- The case is moderately rigid, so you can store gadgets with peace of mind
- Recommended for people like this
  - Remote workers/travelers who want to carry cables and chargers together

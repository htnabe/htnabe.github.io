## How I Found ProBikeKit Japan

Since summer, I've been doing a lot more long rides, so I looked around various sites to upgrade my saddle, bottle, frame bag, and so on.

I didn't know many cheap sites for bike parts, so I used to buy from `Amazon` and `Rakuten` most of the time. But this time I happened to find `ProBikeKit`, and their prices shocked me, so I wrote this post to evangelize `ProBikeKit` to fellow cyclists w

### Items I Bought

I bought these seven items:

1. FIZIK ALIANTE R3 OPEN K:IUM (saddle)
2. TOPEAK SIDEKICK WEDGE SADDLEBAG - MEDIUM (saddle bag)
3. TOPEAK BACKLOADER 6L (saddle bag)
4. CAMELBAK PODIUM CHILL (21 oz bottle)
5. DEDA SQUALO (bar tape)
6. TOPEAK ROAD MORPH G (pump)
7. PBK brand bottle cage

Since it was my first time using ProBikeKit, I ordered only the saddle in my first purchase, then ordered the other six items in my second purchase.

## When I Bought the Saddle (1st Order)

I half-doubted it and ordered only a saddle first, but **it took 3 weeks for the item to arrive**. The timing was right before the Olympics started, so it seems the COVID impact plus increased shipments to Japan during the Olympics made shipping very slow.

I didn't urgently need the saddle, so personally **I was totally fine even with 3 weeks**.

## Happy New Year

**Happy New Year 2021!**

Thank you for your continued support of this blog and `Nabe-cyan` in the year ahead.

## Last Year's Blog and This Year's Goals

This blog launched on March 8th last year, and over those nine months, the **total number of users was approximately 1,500 — or a little over 160 per month.**

When I first started blogging, I was overjoyed on the days when daily visitors broke double digits. Now, a daily weekday average of over 10 is easy to come by.

The most frequently read articles are ranked as follows:

1. Building an Inventory Management System with Slack + GAS + Spreadsheet — Using the Slack API to Log Specific Channels
2. A GAS Beginner's Story of Building a Room Reservation System Using SESAME
3. [Taking the Hokkaido University Graduate School of Engineering Entrance Exam from Another University]({{< relref "HUnivTest.md" >}})
4. Building a Personal Website with Jekyll (Minimal Mistakes) + GitHub Pages, Part 2
5. Home
6. Displaying "Read time" for Japanese Articles in Jekyll

(7th place and below omitted)

The most popular articles — the Slack + GAS + Spreadsheet integration and the GAS + SESAME reservation management system — are the very reason I started this blog (heck yeah).

On the other hand, **the article about failing the Hokkaido University Graduate School entrance exam saw a sudden spike in readers over the last few months**. ~~Especially around the time results were announced lol~~

This year, I'd like to enrich niche tech-related content while also increasing articles about my everyday hobbies.

In particular, [the gas-powered sit-stand desk article]({{< relref "gasdesk.md" >}}) is something I'd like to revisit — there aren't many blogger impressions out there when you search for it, and after using it for about a year, I've discovered new pros and cons worth writing about.

So, this year's goal in a nutshell:

**Casual updates & monetize with ads**

I'll update at a comfortable, sustainable pace, and it's about time I added some non-intrusive ads.

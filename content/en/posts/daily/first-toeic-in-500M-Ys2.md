## Impressions and Lessons Learned

- **There were an insane number of test takers**

I took it on January 29th, and I suspect the high turnout was due to a lot of people taking it for job hunting. Next time I'd like to take it in a month when fewer people show up.

- **The venue was so huge that the sound reverberation was terrible**

There's not much I can do about this, but the sound reverberation was so bad that I couldn't hear the audio as clearly as when I practice listening at home. My desire to take it when fewer people are around went up even more.

- **There weren't that many difficult vocabulary words**

Something that surprised me a little was that I was lucky and barely encountered any words I didn't know.

- **I need to improve my speed-reading skills**

As a personal lesson, I realized I need to sharpen my speed-reading skills for Parts 6 and 7. If I master that, I think my reading score can still go up considerably. Plenty of room for improvement…

- **Practice the full 2-hour mock exam in one sitting**

Obviously the real test is 2 hours long, so I should have at least practiced once with 2 straight hours of problem-solving. Not having done that meant my concentration dropped significantly during the reading section (the last hour) on the actual day.

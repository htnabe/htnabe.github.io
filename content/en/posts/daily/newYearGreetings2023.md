## Last Year's Blog Recap

Last year I wrote a total of 18 articles. The category breakdown was as follows:

- Math: 6
- Tech: 5
- Daily: 7

About 80% of visitors to this blog come to read `Math` category articles on continuum mechanics and solid mechanics. Last year, I set a goal of "writing one continuum mechanics (Math category) article per month."

However, I only managed 6 Math articles — just half of the target. To be honest, this was partly because my job hunting last year didn't go very well, and partly because I simply ran out of topics.

Job hunting is half an excuse, but running out of ideas is the more genuine reason.

I'm not really a fan of writing about obvious, commonly known content. I want to keep writing articles that scratch where it itches — filling in the gaps other sites miss. Sticking to that principle inevitably means spending time digging deep, including checking whether a topic has been covered elsewhere, and researching primary sources.

When you hold yourself to that standard, you can't write **things that come up in 5 seconds of searching**, and you end up stuck for material.

That said, some of the articles I wrote last year — like [Comparison of Bernoulli-Euler Beam and Timoshenko Beam (Theory)]({{< relref "../math/EB-T-beam-method.md" >}}), [Equations of Motion in Cylindrical Coordinates]({{< relref "../math/cylindrical-coordinate-sys.md" >}}), and [Euler's and Cauchy's Equations of Motion [For Beginners]]({{< relref "../math/continuousEquationMotion.md" >}}) — are now getting solid page views and engagement time. I feel like they've hit the mark as **articles that scratch where it itches**, exactly as intended.

One small surprise is that the [Robotics;Notes DaSH Review [Robots are great & Spoiler Warning]]({{< relref "./robonodashreview.md" >}}) article is getting a fair amount of reads too.

It's basically the only game review article on this blog, yet it still gets a decent readership lol

So, going into year 3–4 of running this blog, I'm finally starting to understand what my readers are looking for lol

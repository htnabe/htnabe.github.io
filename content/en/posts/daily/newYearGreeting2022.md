## Last Year's Blog and This Year's Goals

Around October last year, I refreshed the blog design and domain, and the monthly user count still hasn't recovered to what it was before. So this year, my goal is to **surpass the page views from before that redesign**.

To achieve that goal, here are the **top 3 most-read articles from last year**, which serve as an important benchmark:

1. A story about a GAS beginner building a room reservation system using SESAME
2. [Green's Strain Tensor and Infinitesimal Strain Tensor (for Beginners)]({{< relref "../math/strainTensor.md" >}})
3. An Inventory Management System Built with Slack + GAS + Spreadsheet

As you can tell from the top 3 having `GAS`-related articles, readership tends to be proportional to that type of content. That said, unfortunately I personally have no motivation to write more `GAS` articles, so I have no plans to add any. It's more of a _"maybe if I come up with something I want to build"_ kind of feeling…

On a different note, `Green's Strain Tensor` is an outlier — the reality is that _there are virtually no other sites with explanatory articles on such a niche topic_.

Since I personally enjoy topics in `continuum mechanics`, I'm thinking of writing **explanatory articles on continuum mechanics** to boost page views. ~~Is that too obvious a strategy?~~

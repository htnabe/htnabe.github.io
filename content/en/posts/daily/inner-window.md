### Building It

First, try standing the delivered polycarbonate sheets against the window frame.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1709981989/daily/%E5%86%85%E7%AA%93/%E5%86%85%E7%AA%931_l5wjjc.jpg" alt="Polycarbonate sheets leaning against window frame" caption="A perfect fit">}}

Standing them up makes it clear, but the two sheets overlap quite a bit.

So cut away the excess.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1707212150/daily/%E5%86%85%E7%AA%93/%E4%B8%8D%E8%A6%81%E3%81%AA%E9%83%A8%E5%88%86%E3%82%92%E3%82%AB%E3%83%83%E3%83%88_attuvo.jpg" alt="Cutting away the excess portion" caption="Cut">}}

You could work without them, but having a giant cutting mat and giant ruler makes the job so much easier.

Then just cut the window rails to the right length, stick them on with ↓ double-sided tape, and you're done!

## A Simple Experiment

- July 27, 2024, 12pm–2pm, outside temperature 33.2°C, room air conditioner set to 26°C

Method:

1. Place a thermometer between the inner and outer windows for about 1 hour and observe temperature changes
2. Similarly, place a thermometer inside the room and observe temperature changes

If there's a reasonable difference between 1 and 2, I'll take that as evidence that the inner window is having a meaningful effect (simple reasoning)

First, place the thermometer between the inner and outer windows for about 1 hour.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1722066865/daily/%E5%86%85%E7%AA%93/thermo2_e7kxtv.jpg" alt="View of the thermometer from inside the room" caption="Here's how it looks from inside the room">}}

## Finally

When asked if the indoor temperature changed dramatically after installing the double-pane window — in summer, it does feel like there's a perceptible difference! :muscle:

In particular, when you open the inner window to hang laundry, you can definitely feel the warm air escaping.

Though I honestly couldn't tell in winter because the floor in my current room is just cold... :joy_cat:

So it seems best to combine it with floor insulation too.

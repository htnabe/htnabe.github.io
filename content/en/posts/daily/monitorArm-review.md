## Monitor Arm Selection Criteria

Since I didn't know anyone around me who used a monitor arm, I honestly had no idea which one would be good (and I also don't trust store ratings). So this time I actually purchased two different monitor arms:

1. ELECOM monitor arm. Very affordable. Or maybe it's the right price.
2. HP (actually Ergotron) monitor arm. Extremely well-known in the community.

The biggest feature of the ELECOM monitor arm is its price. Compared to an Ergotron arm costing a little over ¥10,000, being able to get a monitor arm for roughly half the price is very attractive.

### Impressions After 2 Weeks of Use

About a week after buying it, I came home one day and noticed the monitor seemed unusually low.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1639194518/NabeBlog/elecom_arm4_hrihqk.webp" alt="elecom_arm_hikui">}}

Upon checking, it turned out I hadn't properly adjusted one of the arm joints, and **the display had been slowly dropping on its own**. Apparently gravity and the arm had been miraculously balanced up until then.

At first I thought "well, it's cheap, loose screws are unavoidable" — but when I looked into it, **the same thing happens with monitor arms from other brands too**, and it seems to be something that inevitably happens over time with extended use. **This is not a defect specific to the ELECOM monitor arm**.

That was the only accident/adjustment issue — no other problems found.

### After 2 Weeks

Several sites have reviewed this monitor arm, and I basically had **exactly the impression I expected**.

One note of caution though: **the clamp is about 1.4× larger than the ELECOM arm's**. You should check in advance that the clamp size fits your desk.

## Final Comparison

Having tried two monitor arms, honestly **I regret not just buying two cheap ELECOM ones**. ELECOM's quality was just that good, and **for most uses the ELECOM monitor arm's specs are more than sufficient**.

So what would be a reason to choose the HP (Ergotron) arm? If I had to say anything, maybe it moves slightly more smoothly than the ELECOM?

That said, I haven't been able to assess durability yet, so my opinion might change in a few years. Take that as you will...

### Update (April 28, 2023)

After more than a year of use, the ELECOM monitor arm had its screws loosen and the monitor shift out of position about 3 times. The HP arm, on the other hand, has had no issues at all.

The ELECOM arm uses a gas spring mechanism, so it's prone to loosening over time.

### Update (December 11, 2023)

Currently I'm using both of these monitor arms heavily, with no particular issues. It's a shame the HP monitor arm seems to be sold out (or discontinued?) on Amazon...

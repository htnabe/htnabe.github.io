This is my second purchase from ProBikeKit, continuing from [last time]({{< relref "proBikeKit-review.md" >}}).

## What I Bought This Time

This time I purchased the Fulcrum Racing 4 (rim brake version) ↓.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1659966348/NabeBlog/wheel_i44bpt.webp" alt="wheel">}}

The ProBikeKit product page is [here](https://www.probikekit.jp/bicycle-wheels/fulcrum-racing-4-c17-clincher-wheelset/11815031.html?variation=11815033).

### Amount Paid

The price at the time of purchase was reduced by ¥3,650 thanks to a ProBikeKit sale and a referral reward, bringing it to ¥35,205[^1], and I paid an additional ¥2,000 in customs duties.

So the total outlay this time came to ¥37,205 — but buying from a domestic Japanese shop would easily run you ¥50,000 or ¥60,000, so it's seriously cheap... lol

### From Order to Receipt

It shipped from the UK within 5 days of ordering.

That's fairly quick for ProBikeKit — they're known for taking a while to ship by default, so if you think it's taking too long, I'd recommend emailing customer service (speaking from experience).

In the end, it only took about a week to receive. I picked it up at the post office, and it was packaged securely ↓ like this. The white bag inside contained the manual and quick release parts.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1659966348/NabeBlog/wheel_case_i5u1mu.webp" alt="wheel_package">}}

### Test Ride and Impressions

I immediately went for a ride of about 100 km, mainly on mountain roads and farm roads.

{{<cldimg url="https://res.cloudinary.com/takenoko/image/upload/v1659966348/NabeBlog/cycle_mountain_qowppv.webp" alt="trial_ride">}}

My previous wheels were actually 23c Fulcrum Racing 7, so not only did I go from 23c to 25c, but the grade went up too — I was expecting _some_ noticeable change in ride feel\*, but honestly I couldn't tell much of a difference before and after the swap lol

I did feel like _the wheels were rolling smoothly_ and _the ratchet sound was pretty quiet_, but I couldn't tell whether it was because the wheels were brand new or because the grade went up.

Also, with the wider tires, the impact from bumps felt a bit softer, but beyond that I genuinely couldn't notice a difference…

By the way, the tires are Panaracer RACE A EVO4. I bought them fresh for this setup.
